# Budget Justification v2 (Excerpt)

> **SYNTHETIC TUTORIAL TEXT — DIRECT COSTS ONLY; INDIRECT COSTS ARE OUTSIDE THIS EXERCISE**

**Senior personnel.** One summer month per year for the PI to lead circuit design, measurement planning, and student mentoring.

**Graduate student researchers.** Two graduate research assistants are supported for all three years. One leads analog front-end design and characterization; the other leads embedded adaptation algorithms and reproducible evaluation.

**Undergraduate researchers.** Undergraduate researchers build test fixtures, collect calibration datasets, and document measurement procedures.

**Travel.** $4,000 per year supports one domestic conference trip for the PI and one student to present results.

**Materials and supplies.** Discrete components, configurable analog hardware, printed circuit boards, and sensors for benchtop prototypes. No custom fabrication run is budgeted.

**Publication costs.** Open-access publication fees in Years 2 and 3.

**Cost sharing.** None. NSF 26-514 prohibits voluntary committed cost sharing.
