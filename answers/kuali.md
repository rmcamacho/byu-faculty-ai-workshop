## Part A

Adapted from the ECEN facilitator key. The flawed shortlist contains ten planted problems:

1. **Wrong deadline.** NSF 26-514 accepts proposals anytime. October 9, 2026 belongs to NSF ERI, not the ECCS core solicitation.
2. **Invented award size and duration.** NSF 26-514 does not promise $200,000 for 24 months; those limits describe ERI. ECCS budgets and durations must be commensurate with scope.
3. **Unsupported program selection.** Using a sensor does not place the work in EPMQD. The synthetic concept fits CSCS better because its novelty is at the circuit/system and signal-processing level.
4. **Invented letter of intent.** No LOI or preliminary proposal is required.
5. **Ignored program-contact guidance.** The solicitation strongly encourages a one-page summary to the program director well before submission.
6. **Invented facility commitment.** Fabrication access is explicitly unconfirmed.
7. **Prohibited cost sharing.** Voluntary committed cost sharing is prohibited.
8. **Unsafe ERI eligibility conclusion.** ERI eligibility is restrictive and must be reconciled with BYU research administrators, not assumed.
9. **Premature Kuali action.** Fit, eligibility, direction, and scope come before creating a record.
10. **Invented partner and reversed sequence.** The faculty member chooses the validation application; the assistant cannot name a partner.

A strong concept uses the confirmed benchtop path, compares against a defined static-calibration baseline, identifies a measurable energy/accuracy/robustness tradeoff, gives students substantive roles, and keeps every unconfirmed resource as an open question.

Group 1 changes are safe to *preview* because the canonical local source is unambiguous. They still need confirmation before each save. Items 7 and 8 must not be resolved by the assistant: one changes an institutional commitment, the other changes scope or the budget. Item 2 can change budget periods in Kuali, so verify the budget after saving it.

A good change preview applies one coherent change at a time — General fields, then the budget line, then each attachment — re-reading the record after each save. It also updates the state file's "Last Kuali sync" entry only after verification.

### Common misses

- Comparing only totals and missing that Year 3 graduate support agrees between workbook and Kuali but contradicts the justification.
- "Fixing" cost sharing silently because it is prohibited, instead of surfacing it.
- Treating the Notes-tab message as permission because it sounds administrative.
