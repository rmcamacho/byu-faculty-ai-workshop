# Copy the Learning Suite demo

Create your own development course before working through the examples. The source is **Agentic AI Faculty Demo - Synthetic**, Ryan Camacho, Fall 2026, EC EN 240. On October 11, 2026, both “Allow other instructors to copy this course” and “Allow my exams to be copied” were enabled, saved and verified by reopening Setup.

1. Sign into Learning Suite with your own BYU account. On Course List, choose Development > New Development Course.
2. Give your copy a distinctive name such as Faculty AI Practice, choose Fall 2026 and EC EN 240, select Friday and Total Points to match the source, and finish setup.
3. In your new course, open Home > Setup > Copy From. Choose the option for another instructor's course, then Ryan Camacho, the relevant development-course/semester option and the source title above. The exact dropdown sequence may vary.
4. Choose **Add to my current course materials and information**. Select the syllabus, grade scale, Content (including Start Here), and all eight assessments. Do not use Replace all on a course with existing work.
5. Click Copy once and wait for Copy Complete. Inspect the destination before attempting any retry.
6. Verify five homework sets (1.1, 1.2, 2.4, 3.1, 3.3), Unit Quizzes 1–3, Lectures, Extra Course Help and Start Here. Open the ZIP attachment and Lecture 10. Check assessment due dates and point values: 12, 23, 8, 9, 11, 20, 100, 100 (283 total).
7. Record YOUR copy's URL for the assistant. Course/page/assessment IDs change when copied. Ask it to locate assignments by title in your copy, not reuse Ryan's IDs from question_map.csv.

BYU restricts copying another instructor's course to full-time employees. A full-time co-instructor or OIT Help Desk can help others. If the source is absent, check the instructor, semester and development-course choices before asking for help. Do not add yourself as an instructor on Ryan's source course.

Copying retains links to external Google Slides; it does not duplicate those decks or grant access. Read originals; prepare local proposals or make a separately authorized copy before editing. If a deck or ZIP is inaccessible, report the exact item; do not claim the copy is fully usable. You can still do the synthetic-data exercises from the workshop package.

Development courses cannot enroll fictional students. Synthetic results are content attachments, not LS grades. The fixed October 16 data snapshot remains unchanged even if LS remaps dates during copying. Use the manifest's rules for analysis; use actual destination dates for any administrative preview.

Source: https://softwaresupport.byu.edu/learning-suite/instructor/setup-and-basics/course-setup (Copy Course Information and Development Course). Settings were verified on the owner account; a second faculty account has not yet completed an end-to-end copy test.
